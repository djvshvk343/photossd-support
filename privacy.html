PhotoSSD Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Introduction

PhotoSSD respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use PhotoSSD.

2. Photo Libraries and External Storage

PhotoSSD accesses photo libraries (.photoslibrary) stored on external storage, such as an external SSD, when selected by the user in order to display photos and videos.

Photos, videos, and photo library content are processed on the user's device to provide the functionality of PhotoSSD.

3. Saving to Apple Photos

When the user chooses to save an item, PhotoSSD may access the Apple Photos library, with the user's permission, to save the selected photo or video.

4. Face ID

PhotoSSD may use device authentication, such as Face ID, to protect access to features such as hidden items. PhotoSSD does not obtain or store biometric data. Authentication is handled through Apple's system-provided authentication services.

5. Data Collection

PhotoSSD does not upload photos, videos, or photo library content read from external storage to servers operated by PhotoSSD.

6. Sharing with Third Parties

PhotoSSD does not sell or provide photos or videos read from external storage to third parties for advertising purposes.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PhotoSSD's features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions regarding PhotoSSD, please email developer.inquiry.5724@gmail.com.

About this website

This website has no custom analytics, advertising, or contact form. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. If you contact us by email, your email address and message are handled to respond to your inquiry. Apple’s policies apply after you follow a link to the App Store.
